FONT LICENSES — Infinite Binary Wobble (iOS)

This application bundles the fonts listed below, used UNMODIFIED under the
SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The fonts retain their original family
names ("Cardo", "Inter"); no Reserved Font Name is reused, so OFL Condition 3
is satisfied. The full, verbatim license text is reproduced once for each
font, beneath that font's copyright notice, as OFL Condition 2 requires.

A Provenance section at the end records the canonical source URL and the
SHA-256 of every shipped font file.
