Privacy Policy

Infinite Binary Wobble · Last updated: June 5, 2026

This policy covers Infinite Binary Wobble — the iOS app, version 1.0, and this website. It is written by J.P. Bowditch, the developer.

What this version collects

This version of the app and this website collect no data. There are no user accounts, no logins, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network connections at all.

Where your gameplay lives

Everything you do happens on your own device. The app stores a small set of gameplay stats — such as your play count and scoreboard — on the device using the system's standard local storage (UserDefaults). That data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Removing the app removes it. Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, change, export, sell, or disclose.

Children

This version collects no data from anyone, including children.

Future versions

Later versions of the app may add optional in-app purchases. If a future version changes what data is involved, this page will be updated first, with a new date above, before that version ships. Your continued use of a later version is covered by the policy as it reads at that time.
