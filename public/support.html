Infinite Binary Wobble

Infinite Binary Wobble is a two-player gravity game for a single device: two people share one phone, pass it back and forth, and try to fling two stars into a stable orbit around each other. Under the hood is a real Newtonian gravity simulation — the orbits you see are the actual physics playing out. It runs entirely offline, with no accounts and no ads.

How to play

  • On the setup screen, drag inside your box to place your star.
  • Set the star's mass with the + / − control.
  • Drag outward from the star to aim and set its launch velocity, then tap Lock In.
  • The second player does the same with their own star.
  • Watch gravity take over: hold a stable two-star orbit to win; a collision or a star flung off into the void is a loss.
